Production

I record all Darksoft material using real instruments in my home studio in Maine. Every part is written, performed, and multitracked by hand, one instrument at a time.

The Studio

  • Guitars: a Fender Stratocaster (Eric Johnson model), recorded DI and re-amped in the mix, plus a Cort acoustic
  • Bass: an Ibanez Gio with flatwound strings
  • Drums: a Premier Artist Maple kit, close-miked with an Audix drum kit and a pair of Shure SM57s on the snare
  • Synths: an Alesis Micron and a Casio Casiotone MT-40
  • Vocals: an AKG C414 condenser
  • Recording: a Universal Audio Apollo Twin into Logic Pro

Plus a pile of found sounds: a theremin, sandpaper, a sword struck with a hammer, and even my basement boiler shutting off.

Process

Albums take shape slowly. Everydayness was recorded over about a year, from November 2024 to December 2025, then mixed and mastered by Brian Fisher.

Many of the music videos are made at home too, shot on a VHS camcorder and an old Nikon Coolpix with practical effects like LED lightboxes, backlit cutouts, and close-up reflections. Watch them on YouTube.
