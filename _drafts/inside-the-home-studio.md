Every Darksoft song is written, performed and multitracked by hand, one instrument at a time, in a home studio.

## The core setup

- **Guitars:** a Fender Stratocaster (Eric Johnson model), recorded DI and re-amped in the mix, plus a Cort acoustic
- **Bass:** an Ibanez Gio with flatwound strings
- **Drums:** a Premier Artist Maple kit
- **Synths:** an Alesis Micron and a Casio Casiotone MT-40
- **Vocals:** an AKG C414 condenser
- **Recording:** a Universal Audio Apollo Twin into Logic Pro
